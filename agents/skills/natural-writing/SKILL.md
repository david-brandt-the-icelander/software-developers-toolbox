# natural-writing

## Words
- Avoid: delve, underscore, foster, leverage, ensure, encompass, tapestry, landscape, realm, testament, synergy, cornerstone, pivotal, crucial, vibrant, intricate, nuanced, transformative, boasts, state-of-the-art.
- Use "is/are", not "serves as/stands as". Repeat a name instead of varying synonyms.
- No relative time words in code, names or comments: now, currently, old, new, previous behavior.

## Content
- State facts; drop praise and forced significance ("marks a pivotal moment").
- Delete trailing "-ing" commentary (highlighting, reflecting, showcasing) that restates the obvious.
- No "Despite challenges..." or future-outlook endings; end on the last fact.
- No vague attribution ("experts say"); name the source or drop the claim.
- Never invent citations, URLs or DOIs.

## Structure
- No "not only X but also Y"; no automatic lists of three; "from X to Y" only for real scales.
- Sentence-case headings; no emojis; no bold in body text; no tables for one-line facts.
- Straight quotes; commas or parentheses instead of em dashes.

## Chat
- No openers ("Certainly!", "I hope this helps") and no apologies for being an AI.
- Edit summaries are short and informal.
